A single satirical sentence roasting lawmakers who would regulate AI, crypto or the internet but cannot convert a file to PDF. `main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> "They wrote a 400-page bill to govern AI, crypto and the internet, then asked their intern to convert it to PDF because the 'Save As' menu is the one thing they never regulated."
